Diablo Carpet360°

Case study

Problem → decisions → product.

01 · Context / problem

The challenge

The product is visual, customized by vehicle, and relatively high-consideration. The campaign needed to generate actual conversations at a low acquisition cost while quickly identifying which creative angle deserved budget.

02 · What I did

The approach

I tested multiple creative angles, used Click-to-WhatsApp as the conversion path, and evaluated delivery based on cost per conversation, spend, and actual message volume rather than surface-level engagement.

03 · Key decisions

What shaped the system

  • Creative angles spanning problem–solution, before–after, and premium positioning
  • Click-to-WhatsApp campaign structure for direct sales inquiries
  • Performance review based on spend, conversation cost, reach, and creative concentration
  • Iterative messaging based on the questions people actually asked after clicking

04 · Final product / result

Where it lands

A compact growth loop where creative, media buying, inbound conversation, and iteration are measured against real sales intent.
