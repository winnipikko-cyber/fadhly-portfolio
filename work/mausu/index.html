Case study

Problem → decisions → product.

01 · Context / problem

The challenge

The goal is not to imitate a huge marketplace. A custom gifting business needs clear product presentation, flexible ordering, trust, and a back office simple enough for a small team to manage every day.

02 · What I did

The approach

The product direction combines a warm storefront with catalog structure, order intent, customer-facing information, and a practical admin experience for products and incoming orders. The system is designed to grow without making the family business feel complicated.

03 · Key decisions

What shaped the system

  • Catalog structure for bouquets, hampers, seserahan, gifts, and custom orders
  • Customer-first ordering flow designed for mobile behavior
  • Admin direction for products, orders, gallery, content, and operational visibility
  • Visual language that feels crafted and personal instead of marketplace-generic

04 · Final product / result

Where it lands

A direct-to-customer commerce system designed around the real way a small custom-gift business operates. The storefront and admin are still being refined before final screenshots are locked into the case study.
