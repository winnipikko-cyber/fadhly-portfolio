SAMAR

Case study

Problem → decisions → product.

01 · Context / problem

The challenge

The core challenge is preserving narrative tension while turning a linear story into a product people can navigate, understand, pay for, and resume without casually exposing paid content.

02 · What I did

The approach

I treat narrative state as product state: story beats, artifacts, paywall boundaries, entitlements, chapter progress, audio, and simple-mode fallbacks become part of one reading system. Monetization and content protection are designed into the architecture rather than bolted on later.

03 · Key decisions

What shaped the system

  • State-based paywall instead of a crude chapter-number cutoff
  • Server-side entitlement model for paid access across devices
  • Immersive artifacts including chat-like scenes, audio, notifications, and story-state transitions
  • Admin/CMS model that protects canon while allowing publication, pricing, and access operations

04 · Final product / result

Where it lands

A story platform whose product mechanics support the narrative instead of flattening it into a generic reader. SAMAR is still being refined, so the portfolio focuses on the product system and decisions rather than premature screenshots.
