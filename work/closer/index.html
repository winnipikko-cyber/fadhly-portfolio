CLOSER

Case study

Problem → decisions → product.

01 · Context / problem

The challenge

Generic CRM interfaces can become data warehouses instead of action systems. The product needs to make ownership, routing, inbox activity, status, and accountability obvious without burying the team in configuration.

02 · What I did

The approach

I shaped CLOSER around lead ownership, reassignment, team routing, sales inboxes, lead detail, close states, follow-up, and performance visibility. The interface is being refined toward a focused operational tool rather than a dashboard full of vanity widgets.

03 · Key decisions

What shaped the system

  • Lead assignment and reassignment flows
  • Team routing and multi-admin / sales ownership logic
  • Inbox, lead detail, status, attachment, and close workflows
  • Theme token system for coherent light/dark product UI without visual drift

04 · Final product / result

Where it lands

A sales-first workspace prototype where the core unit is not a contact record but the next meaningful action on a lead. The product remains in active development.
