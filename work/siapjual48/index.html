SiapJual48

Case study

Turn a vague service into a product people can understand.

01 · Context / problem

The challenge

Small businesses often do not have one isolated design problem. The offer, message, landing page, sales material, and outreach can all be slightly disconnected. A broad “marketing service” makes that worse because the buyer still has to figure out what they actually need.

02 · What I did

The approach

I shaped the service as a short commercial sprint instead of an open-ended agency engagement. The work starts with an audit, collapses the business into one core message, prioritizes the few fixes that matter most, and finishes with assets and next actions the owner can immediately use.

03 · Key decisions

What shaped the system

  • Outcome-led website structure instead of a long list of deliverables
  • A simple audit → core message → execution → ready-to-use workflow
  • One complete demo sprint so prospects can understand the work before buying
  • Clear package boundaries to protect the 48-hour operating promise
  • A prospecting workflow designed around verified leads and personalized outreach

04 · Final product / result

A service that behaves more like a product.

The result is a live commercial system with a public site, a defined sprint, a demo case, prospecting rules, and a repeatable delivery structure. The portfolio focuses on the productization decisions rather than inflating early-stage traction.
